What we do

Four ways to fix a problem

I.

M&A & Transactions

Buying and selling assets and companies end to end: origination, due diligence, valuation, structuring and closing. Full coordination with investment banks, funds and legal advisors.

II.

Divestitures & Carve-outs

Sale of non-core assets, infrastructure and business units. Vendor due diligence, competitive processes and operational separation of the divested asset.

III.

Tender Offers & Special Situations

Public tender and delisting offers, shareholder activism processes and institutional investor relations. Strategy design and full stakeholder management through to closing.

IV.

Strategy & Transformation

Strategic plans, asset rotation and corporate reorganization. Rigorous analysis turned into decisions leadership can actually execute.

Case Files

CONFIDENTIAL CASE FILE "GASLAMP"

Sector · Listed Companies / Tender Offers

Situation. A public tender offer for an IBEX 35 company, with multiple banks, law firms and a tight quorum target across institutional investors in several jurisdictions.

Outcome. Coordinated the shareholder communication campaign, aligned every advisor involved, and closed the deal within the regulatory deadline.

CONFIDENTIAL CASE FILE "GOLD WATCH"

Sector · Telecommunications

Situation. Asset rotation for a telecom operator through the sale of passive infrastructure — towers and 5G sites — to an infrastructure-focused investor.

Outcome. Structured the carve-out, prepared the data room, and negotiated the service agreements needed to keep the network running after closing.

CONFIDENTIAL CASE FILE "ROYALE"

Sector · Telecommunications

Situation. Divestment of an operator's copper network as part of its fiber migration, with regulatory, labor and operational-continuity implications to manage in parallel.

Outcome. Designed the sale process, coordinated technical and legal advisors, and closed in line with the client's strategic timeline.

CONFIDENTIAL CASE FILE "BONNIE"

Sector · Logistics

Situation. Sale of a parcel and last-mile delivery subsidiary in a competitive process, with several bidders and a closing deadline set by the seller's corporate calendar.

Outcome. Ran vendor due diligence, managed the competitive process, and closed with the buyer offering the best terms and continuity guarantees.
